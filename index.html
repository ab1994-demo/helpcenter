Introduction

Welcome to the Help Center. This is your go-to resource for getting answers, browsing documentation, and reaching our support team.

Setting up your account

To get started, create an account using your work email. Once verified, you'll have access to all articles and can raise support tickets.

Browsing articles

Articles are organized by topic. Use the search bar at the top to find answers quickly, or browse by category from the home page.

Raising a support ticket

If you can't find an answer, raise a ticket from the support page. Our team typically responds within one business day.
